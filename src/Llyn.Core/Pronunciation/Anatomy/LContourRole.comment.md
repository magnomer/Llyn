## `public enum LContourRole`

The colour role of one Chao level on a drawn contour.
`LContour.LContourRoleRead` picks it, and every drawing names a colour per role.

## `LContourRoleTop,`

The highest level, level 5.

## `LContourRoleHigh,`

Level 4.

## `LContourRoleMid,`

Level 3.

## `LContourRoleLow,`

Level 2.

## `LContourRoleBottom,`

Level 1, and any level outside the scale.
