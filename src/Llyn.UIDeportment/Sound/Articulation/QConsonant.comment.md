## `internal sealed partial class QArticulation`

The pulmonic consonant chart of the articulation aid.
The chart itself is Conduct's ready `CArticulation`, so the aid only lays it out.

## `private void QConsonantIntroduce()`

Builds the chart's controls once, from the chart `CCatalogConsonantRead` answers.
Rows are manner of articulation and columns are place of articulation.
