# PThemeEtymology.xaml

## `Theme.Etymology.Chip`

One source link: the flag, the headword and its language, with an unlink button only while the field is editable.

## `Theme.Etymology.Entry`

The entry a source word is typed into, which takes the word on Return.

## `Theme.Etymology.Item`

Picks between the chip and the typing entry.

## `Theme.Etymology.Field`

The chips and the entry wrapping as one line of words.

## `Theme.Etymology.Text`

The narrative as the reading view prints it.

## `Theme.Etymology.Prose`

The narrative as the editor takes it, in the size the printed one uses.

## `Theme.Etymology.Menu`

Link and unlink over the narrative, beside the usual clipboard items.

## `Theme.Etymology.Read`

The card the reading view draws, its heading standing above it.

## `Theme.Etymology.Card`

The card the editor draws, its heading inside the head strip as the note card has it.
