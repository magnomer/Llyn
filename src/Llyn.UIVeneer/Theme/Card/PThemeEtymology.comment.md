# PThemeEtymology.xaml

## `Theme.Etymology.Item`

Draws one source link as a chip, or the entry a source word is typed into.
The chip holds the flag, the headword and its language.
Its unlink button shows only while the field is editable.
The entry takes the word on Return.
The field fills the named parts, the commands and the key through `QLookItemAttach`.

## `Theme.Etymology.Field`

The chips and the entry wrapping as one line of words.

## `Theme.Etymology.Text`

The narrative as the reading view prints it.

## `Theme.Etymology.Prose`

The narrative as the editor takes it, in the size the printed one uses.

## `Theme.Etymology.Menu`

Link and unlink over the narrative, beside the usual clipboard items.
Each command is a `QLook` row on the named item.

## `Theme.Etymology.Read`

The card frame the reading view draws, its heading standing above it.

## `Theme.Etymology.Card`

The card frame the editor draws, its heading inside the head strip as the note card has it.

## `Theme.Etymology.Head`

The head strip of the editor card, bordered below and rounded on top.

## `Theme.Etymology.Title`

The heading inside the head strip.

## `Theme.Etymology.Body`

The inset the editor card gives its body below the head strip.
