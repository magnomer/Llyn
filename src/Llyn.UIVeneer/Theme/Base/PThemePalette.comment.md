# PThemePalette.xaml

Defines shared color brushes and font families used by the interface theme.

## `Theme.Ink` and `Theme.Muted`

Provide primary text and subdued text colors from the active theme.

## `Theme.Canvas`, `Theme.Roof`, and `Theme.Navigation`

Provide the window, caption, and navigation background colors.

## `Theme.Surface` and `Theme.SurfaceRaised`

Provide base and elevated surfaces for cards, menus, and hover states.

## `Theme.Line` and `Theme.LineStrong`

Provide standard and emphasized border colors.

## `Theme.Accent`, `Theme.AccentHover`, and `Theme.AccentPress`

Provide the accent color and its hover and pressed variants.

## `Theme.AccentEdge` and `Theme.AccentSoft`

Provide accent border and soft fill colors.

## `Theme.Helper`, `Theme.HelperSoft`, and `Theme.HelperEdge`

Provide the helper color family used by secondary card fields.

## `Theme.Situation`, `Theme.SituationSoft`, and `Theme.SituationEdge`

Provide the situation color family used to distinguish situation fields.

## `Theme.Warning`, `Theme.WarningSoft`, and `Theme.WarningStrong`

Provide warning colors for caution, soft fills, and destructive emphasis.

## `Theme.Favorite` and `Theme.Representative`

Provide semantic colors for favorites and representative entries.

## `Theme.DisabledLine` and `Theme.DisabledInk`

Provide border and text colors for disabled controls.

## `Theme.Pending`

Marks content or actions that remain pending.

## `Theme.Font`

Selects the interface font with system fallbacks.

## `Theme.Phonetic`

Selects fonts for phonetic transcription.

## `Theme.Han`

Selects fonts for Han characters with regional and system fallbacks.
