# Rehearsal

The flashcard tab placeholders.
Its markup carries no hook.
Its subfolders hold the deck list and editor, the cue adder and cell editor, and the template editor.

## `PStudy` and `PScoreboard`

The placeholder pages of the study and statistics tabs, shelled as `PDeck` is.
Each holds one empty line in the catalog's empty style.
  Its text is unset in markup, since the driver looks up the key Conduct names.
