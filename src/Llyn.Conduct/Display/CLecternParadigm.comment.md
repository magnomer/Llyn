# CLecternParadigm.cs

## `public sealed record CLecternParadigm(`

The paradigm block of the reading view for the shown entry, ready to show.

**Parameters**

- `CLecternParadigmSlots`: the first slot of every paradigm row.
- `CLecternParadigmPending`: whether the inflections are still being fetched.
- `CLecternParadigmMorphology`: whether the morphology setting is on, which enables the block.
- `CLecternParadigmFont`: the headword typography of the paradigm's own language.
