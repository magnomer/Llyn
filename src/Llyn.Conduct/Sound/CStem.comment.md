## `public sealed record CStem(long CStemId, string CStemKey, int CStemCount, bool CStemChosen);`

One phonetic series as the xiesheng series column lists it.

**Parameters**

- `CStemId`: the stored series, which a click on the row names.
- `CStemKey`: the series key the row prints.
- `CStemCount`: how many entries carry a character of the series.
- `CStemChosen`: whether the column has this series chosen.
