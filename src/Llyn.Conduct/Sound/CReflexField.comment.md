## `public enum CReflexField`

The cell of a reflex row a user typed into.
The driver maps the cell it heard to one member, and `CTimbre.CTimbreReflexSet` picks the engine member that writes it.

## `CReflexFieldLanguage,`

The language cell of the row.

## `CReflexFieldKind,`

The kind cell of the row.

## `CReflexFieldText,`

The text cell of the row, whether it holds a respelling or a phonetic text.

## `CReflexFieldRomanization,`

The romanization cell of the row.

## `CReflexFieldMeaning,`

The meaning cell of the row.

## `CReflexFieldNote,`

The note cell of the row.
