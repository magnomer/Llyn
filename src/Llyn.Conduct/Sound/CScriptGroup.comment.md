# CScriptGroup.cs

## `public sealed record CScriptGroup(`

The forms of one character in one style, as the script panel groups them.

**Parameters**

- `CScriptGroupHeading`: the character the group shows.
- `CScriptGroupStyle`: the script style.
- `CScriptGroupGloss`: the gloss of the style.
- `CScriptGroupImages`: the forms, in the pack's order.
