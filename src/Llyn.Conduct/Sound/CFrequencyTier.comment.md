## `public enum CFrequencyTier`

The tier of an entry's frequency band, as a closed set the chip paints.
`LDisplay` maps the engine's rank name into it by name.
Any name it does not know becomes `CFrequencyTierUnknown`, so the chip always finds its brush and its name.
