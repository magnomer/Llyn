# TXieshengVista.cs

## `public sealed class TXieshengVista`

Covers the xiesheng panel's vista restore, its observers, its order menu and its close on a real workspace.
A Workspace notice unchooses the series, empties the entry list and tells the driver once.
A Fanqie or Settings notice raises the column and the entry list.
An Entry notice also raises the panel.
A second restore carries the held queries into the fresh vistas.
After a second restore a Fanqie notice raises the column once, through the marshal.
The menu offers the three orderings.
The atelier's close lets the editor's draft go and stops the recording.
