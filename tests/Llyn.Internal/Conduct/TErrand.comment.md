# TErrand.cs

## `public sealed class TErrand`

Covers the searches a desk runs and the maps between engine steps and their Conduct shapes.
A recording travels both ways unchanged, since the clip saves and plays what it listed.
A reading travels out unchanged, and a missing recording or reading maps to none.
With no tenure held, neither search starts, and the clip answers its empty notice.
Each recording step changes the clip first and raises it ready, once per step.
Each reading step raises its own event: a starting source, a find, or the end.
Rows keep the pack's order once each, and a row without an address says missing or broken.
An empty answer after a recording keeps the recording, and an untagged one carries a blank flag key.
A pick saved or previewed while no recording search runs does nothing, and every reading-search read answers empty.
