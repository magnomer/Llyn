# TForay.cs

## `public sealed class TForay`

A foray as the tenure hands it out: its search, the cancel that ends it, and the pick that follows.
A cancelled search never tells its listener it finished, so the menu is not told a lie.
A pick after the headword moved attaches nothing, and one over an unchanged draft attaches the audio.
A cancelled tenure takes its foray down with it.
The gated client holds every source open until the test releases it.
