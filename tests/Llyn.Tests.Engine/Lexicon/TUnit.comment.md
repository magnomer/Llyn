## `public sealed class TUnit`

Covers an entry's lexical unit and freedom, kept apart from its parts of speech.
It covers both through save and reload, and through a markup round trip.
It covers a retired unit in markup, which is ignored.
It covers the units and freedoms offered, the same in every language.
It covers a language change, which leaves both as chosen.
It covers the dropdown's second choice of the held value, which clears it.
It covers the unit and freedom chips that lead the speech chips of a Joplin note.
