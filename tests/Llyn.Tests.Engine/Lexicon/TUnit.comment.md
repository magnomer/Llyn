## `public sealed class TUnit`

Covers an entry's lexical unit, kept apart from its parts of speech.
It covers the unit through save and reload, and through a markup round trip.
It covers the units a language offers by its spacing flag, and a unit settled when the language changes.
It covers the dropdown's second choice of the held unit, which clears it.
It covers the unit chip that leads the speech chips of a Joplin note.
