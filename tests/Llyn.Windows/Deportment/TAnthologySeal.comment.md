# TAnthologySeal.cs

## `public sealed class TAnthologySeal`

Covers the maps the sealed corpus controllers draw from the engine's records to Conduct shapes.
A missing Example maps to none, and a stored one carries every field.
The excerpt links its Mentions only while the text reads soundly, and the transcript keeps them always.
A clicked Mention carries its entry and sense.
These move to the portable suite once the panel sinks into Conduct.
