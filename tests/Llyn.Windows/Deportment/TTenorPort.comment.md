# TTenorPort.cs

## `public sealed class TTenorPort`

The sealed tenor controller over fake entry and settings ports, with no engine and no database behind it.
With no vista restored the rows are empty and nothing is asked, since the stub port would throw.
A creation and a language read reach the port they belong to and nothing else.
A creation answers only the stored id.
With nothing chosen and nothing shown, New names a register.
The register rows the engine lists come out as Conduct copies with their usage and chosen mark.
