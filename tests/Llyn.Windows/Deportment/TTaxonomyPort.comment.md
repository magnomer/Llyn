# TTaxonomyPort.cs

## `public sealed class TTaxonomyPort`

The sealed taxonomy controller over fake entry and settings ports, with no engine and no database behind it.
With no vista restored the rows are empty and nothing is asked, since the stub port would throw.
A tag creation reaches the entry port and answers only the stored id.
With nothing chosen and nothing shown, New names a tag.
An empty list reads as vacant under a blank search and as unmatched under a written one.
The tag rows the engine lists come out as Conduct copies with their chosen mark.
These move to the portable suite once the controller sinks into Conduct.
