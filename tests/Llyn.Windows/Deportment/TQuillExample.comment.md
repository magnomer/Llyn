# TQuillExample.cs

## `public sealed class TQuillExample`

Covers the text gate over an Example desk, the corpus transcript's every edit.
Typed text lands as known even over an unknown value, and marks the draft changed.
A picked language and a picked Source land at once.
A Gloss is added, retyped, relanguaged and dropped by its id.
A Mention is added over a span, pointed at a sense and dropped by its id.

## `private static LDesk TQuillExampleStart(LEngine engine, LStateValue text)`

Stores an English Example with the given text and starts a desk on it from the corpus origin.
