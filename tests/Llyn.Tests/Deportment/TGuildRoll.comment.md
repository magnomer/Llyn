# TGuildRoll.cs

## `public sealed class TGuildRoll`

Covers the roll of the authors panel and the areas a chosen row opens.
An uncredited Source puts the orphan row first, and a query drops it with the Authors it does not match.
A chosen Author shows the vita with its worded counts, its fellows, and its Sources in the oeuvre.
The orphan row lists the uncredited Sources and leaves the mode and bin dead.
A Source chosen in the oeuvre shows the colophon and takes the mode away.
