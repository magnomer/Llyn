## `internal static class TInterfaceConductDialog`

The relays for Conduct's dialog rules.
They build the markup import's customs sheet and reach its rows, and they reach the coinage wording rule.
No relay builds a WPF window, so the rules are tested without a dialog.
Each relay is transparent and carries no test logic of its own.
